My Love…

Happy Birthday, meri jaan. ❤️

Aaj ka din sirf isliye special nahi hai kyunki aaj tumhara birthday hai.

Mere liye aaj ka din isliye special hai kyunki isi din is duniya mein woh ladki aayi thi jo ek din meri puri duniya banne wali thi.

Kabhi kabhi main sochta hoon ki agar tum meri life mein nahi aati, toh shayad meri life chal toh rahi hoti… lekin woh life meri wali life nahi hoti.

Usme woh excitement nahi hoti jo tumhara message dekh kar hoti hai. Woh smile nahi hoti jo tumhari ek chhoti si baat se automatically aa jaati hai. Woh sukoon nahi hota jo tumse baat karne ke baad milta hai.

Aur shayad mujhe kabhi pata bhi nahi chalta ki kisi ek insaan ka mere liye itna important ho jaana possible hai.

Tum meri life ka sirf ek part nahi ho.

Tum meri life ki woh jagah ho jahan main baar-baar lautna chahta hoon.

Tumse baat karna mera favourite part hai. Tumhe hasana mujhe khud khush kar deta hai.

Tum naraz hoti ho toh sach mein sab kuch thoda ajeeb sa lagne lagta hai. Tum khush hoti ho toh bina reason mera mood bhi achha ho jaata hai.

Aur jab tum mujhe apna samajh kar apni chhoti se chhoti baat batati ho na… pata nahi kyun, lekin us waqt mujhe duniya ka sabse lucky insaan hone jaisa feel hota hai.

Mujhe tumhari perfect version nahi chahiye. Mujhe tum chahiye.

Tumhari hasi. Tumhara gussa. Tumhari zid. Tumhare silly moments. Tumhari overthinking. Tumhari random baatein. Tumhara bina kisi reason ke mood change ho jaana. Tumhara care karna.

Tumhara woh tareeka jisme kabhi kabhi tum kuch bolti nahi ho, lekin phir bhi bahut kuch keh deti ho.

Mujhe ye sab chahiye. Kyuki ye sab milkar tum banti ho. Aur mujhe tumse hi pyaar hai. ❤️

Hamari story perfect nahi hai. Aur shayad mujhe perfect story chahiye bhi nahi. Mujhe hamari story chahiye.

Woh jisme hasi bhi hai. Fights bhi hain. Misunderstandings bhi hain. Pagalpan bhi hai. Long conversations bhi hain. Kabhi chhoti si baat par mood off hona bhi hai. Aur phir ek dusre ke bina zyada der reh na paana bhi hai.

Main hamari har memory ko isliye yaad nahi rakhta kyunki woh sab perfect moments the. Main unhe yaad rakhta hoon kyunki un moments mein tum thi.

Aur sach bolun toh tumhare saath ordinary moment bhi mere liye memory ban jaata hai.

Ek chhote se hello se shuru hui baat kab itni gehri ho gayi, pata hi nahi chala.

Kabhi kabhi ek normal conversation. Ek random photo. Ek chhota sa joke. Ek call. Ek “kya kar rahe ho?” Ek “khana kha liya?”

Ye sab bahar se bahut normal lagta hai. Lekin mere liye nahi.

Mere liye ye woh chhoti chhoti cheezein hain jinse meri life beautiful bani hai.

Tumne meri life mein sirf memories nahi add ki. Tumne meri life ko feel karne ka tareeka change kar diya.

Tumne mujhe kisi ka wait karna sikhaya. Kisi ki awaaz miss karna sikhaya. Kisi ki smile ko apni khushi samajhna sikhaya.

Aur sabse zyada… tumne mujhe ye samjhaya ki “home” hamesha koi jagah nahi hoti.

Kabhi kabhi home ek insaan hota hai.

Aur mere liye woh insaan tum ho. ❤️

Main nahi jaanta future exactly kaisa hoga. Kaunse din easy honge. Kaunse difficult. Kitni baar hum ladenge. Kitni baar ek dusre ko samajhne mein time lagega.

Lekin ek cheez hai jo main jaanta hoon.

Main har version of future mein tumhe choose karna chahta hoon.

Good days mein bhi. Bad days mein bhi. Exciting days mein bhi. Boring days mein bhi. Jab life bahut beautiful lage tab bhi. Aur jab sab kuch complicated lage tab bhi.

Main tumhare saath sirf best moments nahi chahta. Main tumhare saath real life chahta hoon.

Woh life jisme subah ke normal days hon. Late night baatein hon. Random plans hon. Cancelled plans hon. Photos hon. Fights hon. Sorry ho. Hugs hon. Dreams hon. Aur har baar kisi bhi problem ke baad hum dono fir se ek dusre ke paas aa jaayein.

Main tumhe promise nahi kar sakta ki main hamesha perfect rahunga. Main mistakes karunga. Kabhi stupid behave karunga. Kabhi tumhe samajhne mein late karunga. Kabhi shayad bina matlab tumhe irritate bhi karunga.

Lekin ek promise main dil se kar sakta hoon.

Main hamesha try karta rahunga. Tumhe samajhne ke liye. Tumhe khush rakhne ke liye. Tumhari respect karne ke liye. Tumhare dreams ko support karne ke liye.

Aur jab tumhe lage ki duniya thodi zyada heavy ho gayi hai… tab tumhare saath khade rehne ke liye.

Main chahta hoon tum apne life mein bahut kuch achieve karo. Bahut travel karo. Bahut hanso. Apne saare dreams complete karo. Woh sab bano jo tum banna chahti ho.

Aur kabhi bhi apne aap ko chhota mat samajhna. Kyuki jis ladki ko main dekhta hoon na… woh bahut special hai. Bahut strong hai. Bahut beautiful hai. Aur woh apne aap se jitna expect karti hai, usse kahin zyada deserve karti hai.

Aur haan… beautiful se mera matlab sirf tumhari smile ya tumhari photos nahi hai.

Tum beautiful ho tumhare heart ki wajah se. Tumhare care karne ki wajah se. Tumhare emotions ki wajah se. Tumhare us soft side ki wajah se jo tum har kisi ko nahi dikhati. Aur tumhare us pagal side ki wajah se bhi jo mujhe bahut pasand hai. ❤️

Aaj tumhara birthday hai. Aur usually birthdays par log wish karte hain ki tumhari saari wishes complete ho. Main bhi wahi wish karta hoon.

Lekin thoda selfish hoke ek aur wish karta hoon.

Ki chahe life kitni bhi change ho jaaye… chahe years pass ho jaayein… chahe hum dono kitne bhi bade ho jaayein… mere paas tumhe isi tarah birthday wish karne ka haq hamesha rahe.

Main har saal tumhe dekh kar ye keh sakun: “Happy Birthday, meri jaan.”

Aur har saal mere paas tumse pyaar karne ke aur zyada reasons ho.

Tum meri favourite person ho. Meri favourite notification. Meri favourite awaaz. Meri favourite smile. Meri favourite memory. Mera favourite future.

Aur agar mujhe dobara beginning se choose karna pade… toh main phir tumhe hi choose karunga.

Har baar. Without doubt. Without thinking twice. Sirf tum. ❤️

Thank you meri life mein aane ke liye. Thank you mere pagalpan ko tolerate karne ke liye. Thank you mujhe care dene ke liye. Thank you meri memories ka sabse beautiful part banne ke liye. Aur thank you sirf tum hone ke liye.

Happy Birthday, meri cute wife. ❤️

I love you more than main kabhi properly words mein explain kar paunga. Aur shayad isi liye maine ye poora experience banaya. Kyuki ek simple “Happy Birthday” kabhi enough tha hi nahi.

Tum mere liye simple nahi ho. Tum special ho. Bahut zyada.

Jo kuch peeche tha woh hamari kahani hai... aur jo aage hai, use bhi main tumhare saath hi jeena chahta hoon.

Aur ye story yahin khatam nahi hoti. Abhi toh humne bas kuch memories banayi hain. Baaki poori life padi hai.

aur main chahta hoon ki usmein har possible jagah… tum mere saath ho. ❤️

Always yours.
