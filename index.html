Happy Birthday, My Love ❤️

Aaj ka din mere liye sirf tumhara birthday nahi hai… yeh woh din hai jis din meri duniya ko tum mili thi. Kabhi kabhi main sochta hoon ki meri zindagi tumse pehle kaisi thi, aur phir mujhe samajh aata hai ki shayad kuch cheezein tab bhi adhuri thi, bas mujhe pata nahi tha ki unhe poora karne wali tum hogi. Tum meri zindagi ka woh khoobsurat hissa ho jise main sirf pyaar nahi karta, balki har din mehsoos karta hoon. Tumhari smile, tumhari baatein, tumhara gussa, tumhari care, tumhari choti choti aadatein, aur tumhara mere saath hona… in sab ne meri life ko ek aisi kahani bana diya jise main baar baar jeena chahta hoon. Main tumhe har baar perfect words mein explain nahi kar paata, lekin ek baat dil se jaanta hoon — tum mere liye bahut special ho. Tumhare saath bitaya hua har moment mere liye ek memory hai, aur main chahta hoon ki aane wale har saal mein hum aur bhi zyada memories banayein. Aaj tumhara birthday hai, lekin honestly, gift mujhe mila tha jis din tum meri life ka part bani. Isliye aaj main sirf tumhe “Happy Birthday” nahi kehna chahta. Main tumhe thank you kehna chahta hoon. Mere saath rehne ke liye. Mujhe samajhne ke liye. Meri life ko itna beautiful banane ke liye. Aur sabse zyada… meri zindagi mein “tum” hone ke liye. I hope tumhari aankhon mein hamesha wohi sparkle rahe, tumhari smile kabhi kam na ho, aur tumhari har wish dheere dheere reality ban jaye. Aur agar kabhi life difficult ho, to bas itna yaad rakhna — main tumhare saath hoon. Aaj, kal, aur jitne bhi birthdays hum saath celebrate kar sakein. Happy Birthday, meri jaan. I love you. ❤️
